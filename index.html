Mawasi Tactical Standard Operating Procedures
🔥 Mawasi Tent Fire Contingency Protocol
1. Sound whistle or emergency horn immediately.
2. If front entrance blocked, cut tent fabric on opposing side with utility knife.
3. Evacuate children upwind away from heavy plastic smoke.
4. Deploy Dry Powder extinguishers (PASS rule) and wet sand barrels on base of flame.
🌙 Anti-Intrusion & Night Camp Lockdown Protocol
1. Total perimeter lockdown at 17:30 daily.
2. Verify solar floodlights illuminated around girls' and infants' quarters.
3. Guards execute internal patrols every 45 minutes.
4. ZERO WEAPONS POLICY strictly enforced at gate without exception.
📡 Complete Telecom Blackout Action Plan
1. Freeze all inter-governorate road movements immediately.
2. Stationary check-in window times: 09:00 and 15:00 at camp gate.
3. Rely on laminated paper emergency cards for designated medical points in Mawasi.
